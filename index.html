Recent milestone

Rebuilt a live, revenue-bearing app onto a new stack with zero downtime and no data loss.

GroomBook is a live grooming-business platform with a paying customer. I rebuilt it from a single-file static app onto a modern stack — Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Supabase (Postgres) on Vercel — then cut production over with no data loss. The migration ran from a written runbook as a reversible, gated process: pre-cutover backups, row-count drift checks that halted the run when data moved, an atomic migration, an eight-point verifier, and a rollback path that was never needed.

v2 evolves the product toward multi-persona access and enterprise use rather than replacing what already worked. The wider vision lives in KoyaOS, my multi-venture operating system. Re-platforming a revenue-bearing product without breaking the business is the credential I am most proud of.
