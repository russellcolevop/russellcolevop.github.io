# Russell Works: desktop scroll mode (2026-10-01)

Scope: `works/` (walk.js, works.css, rooms.json), HANDOFF.md and this report. Root site, hub, profiles, build-profiles.py, corbel-site and the one-page version untouched. `/works/` is not linked from the root. Committed on main, not pushed (origin/main was f830ce8 at the start: `git rev-list --left-right --count origin/main...HEAD` was `0 0`).

Owner instruction: desktop should be a scrolled experience like the Corbel Ops 3D site, not Matterport-style drag-to-look. Reference read from `corbel-site/spatial/` (read only, nothing run).

## What changed
- **Mode.** One predicate, `(min-width: 900px) and (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, evaluated in `createWalk` and watched live. When it holds, `html.scrolltour` is set and everything below applies; when it does not (phones, touch tablets, 760 to 899 px) nothing below exists in the DOM and Next, Back, the peek rail, tilt and drag behave exactly as before. Crossing the breakpoint in a live window switches mode in place and re-seats the camera on the same stop.
- **Scroll track.** One empty spacer after `#walk` (outside it, because `.walk` is fixed) is `(0.8 + 20)` viewport heights tall: 0.8 of elevator, then one viewport per stop of the existing 20-stop `tour`. `s = (scrollY - 0.8 vh) / vh`. The stop whose section covers the middle of the viewport (`round(s)`) owns the plate, the journey bar and the room. The camera is `between(floor(s), floor(s) + 1, frac(s))` clamped to the current room's stops, so yaw, pitch and fov move continuously between stops inside a room. When `round(s)` enters another room the existing fade walk runs and lands on that stop. Scroll events only set a flag; the work happens inside the existing render frame (no second rAF, no loop at rest).
- **Plates.** One real `<section>` per stop inside `#walk`, left, `min(380px, 38vw)`, translucent paper (the same plate look as the popup and cards), vertically centred, never docked, scrolls inside itself only if taller than the viewport minus 230 px. Eyebrow `05 / 11 · AI WORKSHOP`. A room's first stop carries its sheet copy (heading, paragraph, proof line, actions); every other stop carries the cards of the exhibits it frames (the same text their card shows), minus a card that repeats the sheet (product sheet 1). Actions reuse `actionEl`: the sheet's links and buttons except "Next: ..." and "Back to ..." (the journey bar's job), a card's link (Try the public demo), and, on the last plate (contact) only, "One-page version" (the phone's last Next; the bar's next is disabled there). Inactive plates are `inert`, `aria-hidden` and `visibility:hidden` (as Corbel does), so Find and screen readers meet only the current plate; they crossfade (0.2 s out, 0.3 s in). Existing copy only.
- **Journey bar.** Replaces the floating pill on desktop: bottom, 52 px, paper at 90% with blur and hairline top and bottom edges. Position `05 / 11 Workshop`, eleven numbered chapter buttons (current one filled, name in `title` and `aria-label`), previous and next stop buttons (next disabled on the last stop; previous on the first stop goes to the elevator), progress line at its foot that tracks `s` continuously. Hidden in the elevator. The peek rail is `display:none` in this mode.
- **Hint.** The existing `#whint` pill reads "Scroll to explore ↓" on the first screen and fades on the first scroll (or on leaving the elevator).
- **Framing under the plate.** `framing()` takes a left inset (the plate's right edge plus 28 px, measured, `SCRL.left`): stops are fitted to the clear area to its right, with the yaw shifted by `atan((L / H) tan(fov / 2))` and the fill taken against the clear width. Arrival frames get the same shift (Russell on the reception floor lands right of the plate). The elevator keeps its own unshifted frame.
- **Input.** The canvas wheel zoom is off in this mode so the wheel scrolls the page; arrow keys are no longer taken for look-around, so Page Down, Space, arrows, Home and End scroll the page natively. Drag still looks around. The next scroll tick eases whatever was dragged, panned or keyed away from the scripted view with a 70 ms time constant (about 400 ms), held while a pointer is down; the loop stops itself.
- **Navigation.** In this mode `walkTo` converts any call into a scroll to the room's first stop, so doorways, map dots, header Contact and brand, popup choices, sheet actions, chapter buttons and the room tag all use one path. A click that crosses rooms shows the target room at once and the scroll glides there afterwards (`aim`), so the rooms between are not walked through (no pano loads for them); a wheel, key or touch cancels it. A scroll that races a walk is re-checked when the walk ends; a pano that fails to load is not retried until the next scroll. Doorway, chapter and popup choices push a history entry; scrolling replaces the hash with the current room. Back and Forward scroll to that room's first stop. A deep link `#room` opens at that room's first stop; `scrollRestoration` is manual while the mode is on and restored on exit. The next room's panorama is warmed on arrival. While the map dialog is open the world ignores the scroll and catches up on close.
- **Reduced motion.** No interpolation: each stop snaps when its section reaches the middle of the viewport; no drag easing; scrolls to a stop are instant.
- **Product stop 0 on a phone.** At 375 x 664 the painted "Fuwari" heading sat under the room tag and the caption chip (at 812 it only just cleared). Cause: the group the stop frames is the monitor plus the caption's anchor point, which excludes the caption's own box and the sheets above, so the frame sat too high. New optional per-stop `up` (degrees to look up on a phone) and `upScroll` (the same in the scroll tour) in rooms.json: product stop 0 `up: 7, upScroll: 4`. Phone frame pitch -12.5 becomes -5.5; fov unchanged (78.8 at 812, 67.8 at 664) so the painted type sizes are untouched. All eleven rooms' stop 0 were then checked at 375 x 812 and 375 x 664: no painted text under the masthead (0 to 60 px), room tag (62 to 92) or caption chip (96 to 115). No other room needed a change.
- Size: walk.js +104 / -15, works.css +29 / -2 (plate, journey bar, spacer overrides, a `:has` rule that hides the plate while an object card is open), rooms.json 1 line. No new dependency, no index.html change (plates and bar are built by walk.js, only in this mode).

## Judgement calls
- **Plate switch at the halfway point** (`round`, as Corbel does), so the plate, bar and room change halfway through a pan, not at its end. Single-stop rooms hold their frame for a full viewport of scroll.
- **Elevator lead-in is 0.8 of a viewport**, which with the midpoint rule means the elevator holds for the first 0.3 viewport (about 270 px at 900 high, three wheel clicks) and reception holds the next full viewport. The popup and doors are unchanged.
- **One spacer, not 20 sections.** Same geometry; a single element sized in px is the smaller diff and the reduced-motion midpoint rule is arithmetic.
- **A chapter click across rooms shows the target at once and glides the scrollbar behind it** rather than gliding through the intermediate rooms. The alternative walks (fade plus a panorama load) through every room in between.
- **Next and Back to are not repeated in the plates** because the journey bar carries them.
- **While an object card is open the plate hides** (CSS `:has`; browsers without it overlap instead). The card opens beside its object as before, which on a narrow desktop lands over the plate's place; the plate repeats the same text anyway.
- **The room tag, masthead and caption are unchanged.** The caption chip sits 9 px below the journey bar at 1024 and 1440. The room tag now jumps to the room's first stop when clicked in this mode (its old job, reopening the rail, has no rail).
- **Desktop product stop 0 is nudged 4 degrees (`upScroll`), not the phone's 7.** Un-nudged, the background sheets' painted text sat under the Map, Contact and One-page chips at 1024 and 1440 (seen in the shipped-frame screenshot). 7 degrees clears the chips at 1024 but leaves the monitor caption 3 px above the journey bar at 1440 x 900 and would overlap the bar on shorter windows (the bar's height is fixed). 4 degrees: caption 73 px above the bar at 1024 x 768 and 55 px at 1440 x 900, sheet text clear of the chips at 1024. At 1440 x 900 the first lines of the third background sheet still sit partly under the One-page chip; closing that needs a wider (zoomed-out) frame, not more pitch, and was not done.
- **Workshop bench pins and similar low hotspots can sit behind the journey bar** at stops framed low (the bar is full width where the pill was 480 px). They stay in the tab order and pan into view on focus.
- **Inert plates mean Find only reaches the current plate.** A deliberate trade for a clean reading order.
- **Thin scrollbar** (`scrollbar-width: thin`) on the page in this mode; the page's own scrollbar is kept as an affordance.

## Not verified
Safari and WebKit (`inert`, `:has`, `scrollbar-width`, smooth `scrollTo` and the `scrollend`-free landing check were only run in Chromium), real trackpad momentum (wheel events were discrete 100 px ticks and instant jumps; a fling across several rooms was only simulated by the 15-jump race), VoiceOver and other screen readers on the plates and bar, Lighthouse, a classic always-visible scrollbar (the headless runs showed none, so the 15 px of lost width was not seen), viewports shorter than 768 px high on desktop (the plate scrolls inside itself; not looked at), a touchscreen laptop that matches the desktop query (touch-action is `pan-y`; not tried), the pane's smooth glide for Back and Forward on a real display, a real iPhone, and the phone product frame on a device with different safe areas. Everything above was re-run on the final code (headless Chrome, device pixel ratio 1 except the one Retina sweep); the pane checks marked (pane) were run on code a few edits earlier (the elevator camera, chapter history, `pos` shadowing and plate-focus fixes are in the final code; the One-page action and the product nudges came after).
